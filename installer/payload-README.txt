White Bag Puppy (installed by White Bag Puppy.exe)

This folder is where White Bag Puppy lives: its settings, loot history and logs are all here.
It's in your AppData folder so OneDrive, Windows' folder protection and zip files can't get in the way.

Open it with the White Bag Puppy shortcut on your Desktop or in the Start menu.
To update: the pup updates itself, or download the newest White Bag Puppy.exe and open it.
To remove it: delete this folder and the two shortcuts.

It comes with its own Java (the "runtime" folder, Eclipse Temurin / OpenJDK, GPLv2 with the Classpath Exception;
its licences are in runtime\legal), so you don't need to install Java.
